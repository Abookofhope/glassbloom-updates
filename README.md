# Glassbloom for Android

On an Android phone, tap **[Download Glassbloom](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Abookofhope/glassbloom-updates/main/glassbloom.apk)**,
then open the file to install. A newer file installs over an older one and keeps all your progress.

If the download keeps loading and never finishes, open this page in Chrome itself, not inside another
app, then hold the link and choose **Download link**.

Once the app is installed, it fetches its own updates while you play: close and reopen it to continue on the
new version.

The app checks [version.json](version.json) here and says when a newer version is ready.

## If Google Play Protect warns about it

Glassbloom isn't on the Play Store, and Google hasn't seen this app's maker before, so Android shows its
"unknown app" screen. That is a general screen for any app that comes from outside the Play Store, not a
report that something harmful was found. The permissions it asks for are the internet (to look for new
versions), vibration (for the haptics) and installing its own updates. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing leaves
your phone unless you press Send yourself in your own email app. Tap **Got it** to cancel at no cost, or
**Install anyway** if you trust where the file came from.
